## A collectors realization

Like a lot of millennials, I have an unhealthy addiction to collecting physical media. For most of my life, hunting for physical media has been my most consistent hobby, and I’ve been fortunate enough to build a collection that spans thousands of video games, books, and records.

Sometime in 2023 I started noticing a growing disconnect between the joy of acquiring things and the time I actually spent with them. That tension made me rethink what collecting really meant to me. When I was younger the goal was always to collect as much physical media as possible to entertain myself for the rest of my life, but as I get older there's an existential dread that creeps in everytime I walk into my game room filled to the brim with games and books I've never played.

However, it wasn't until 2025 that I decided to do someting about it, to be more intentional with the things I collect and how I consumed them. My space is finite, but more than that, I wanted collecting to shift from buying things to experiencing them. To keep myself accountable I started logging what I played and read in a simple travelers notebook and gave each item thing I consumed a numerical score.
